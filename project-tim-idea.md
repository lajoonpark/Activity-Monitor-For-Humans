Lajoon is planning a fork of this repo called TIM ("The Ironic Monitor"): a flashy, video-game-inspired activity monitor. Full notes live in their Obsidian vault at `App ideas/TIM.md` (iCloud Obsidian path). Key elements: animated stick-man mascot Tim Howard (bored employee, dreams of moving to Italy), off-screen characters (boss Jimmy "the boss" Nelson, coworkers) who speak via speech bubbles from the window edge, heavy easter eggs, and a landing page narrated by Tim.

**Why:** Wants something fun and satisfying ("could sit there all day clicking") rather than the lightweight utilitarian MacExplainer direction. The data/metrics engine from MacExplainer should be reused; the personality layer is the new work.

**How to apply:** Design decision made 2026-10-07: Tim is an animated *overlay* on a normal stats UI (a persistent stage strip where he walks, reacts to metrics, and banters with off-screen Jimmy) — NOT a full office scene where stats are props. The scene approach was rejected as flashier but too heavy and distracting from the app's purpose. Keep stats front and center; personality is commentary.
