Wren is the product vision for this app: a calm, nature-inspired macOS activity monitor. The name comes from the wren — a small, quiet bird with an outsized song. The app watches silently and speaks up only when something matters. It answers the everyday questions honestly (*"Is my Mac running normally?"*, *"What is making my Mac feel slow?"*, *"Is high RAM usage actually a problem?"*) and stays out of the way otherwise.

**Why:** Rebrand decided 2026-10-08 (replacing the earlier flashy, game-inspired concept): Lajoon wants a quieter product — light, restrained, and trustworthy rather than performative. The earlier mascot/cast idea (stick-man employee, off-screen boss, speech bubbles, easter eggs) was dropped entirely.

**How to apply:** Design direction is calm, light, nature-inspired. Keep the **Paper & Panel** visual language (warm paper, soft ink, quiet panels) and let it carry the calm identity. Stats stay front and center. Motion is gentle and rare — nothing bounces, pops, or flashes; respect Reduce Motion. No mascot, no characters, no fiction layer. The personality is in the writing: plain, warm, occasionally wry, never dramatic. Calm about what is normal, plain about what is worth knowing, never alarming for effect.
